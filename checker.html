Contract checker · Coming soon

Check a contract against the law

Upload a contract, a tax or enforcement notice, or a letter that cites Sri Lankan law. See whether each Act and section it relies on exists, is still in force and says what the document claims, from the official text.

What it will check

A sense check against the official text, not legal advice.

  • The law cited existsThe Act, number, year and section are real, and the section says what the document quotes.
  • It still appliesThe Act has not been repealed, and amendments since are flagged, with what they changed.
  • The numbers add upPenalties, rates, thresholds and deadlines are within what the Act allows.
  • Questions to askAnything unclear is listed, with the official wording to take to your lawyer or accountant.
