Bills

Is Parliament getting laws made?

Every government bill and where it stands: which became law, which are moving, which have stalled and which were dropped. Bills near a final vote, and those already law, show what they change.

Loading bills.

"Stalled" means a bill has not moved for 90 days or more. Bills only end when the government withdraws them, Parliament votes them down, or Parliament is dissolved; until then a stalled bill can still come back. What each bill changes is drawn from the text published by Parliament or the Government Printer. The official text is what counts.
