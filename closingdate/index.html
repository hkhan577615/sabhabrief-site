    What the salary codes mean
    PL
    Primary level: drivers, office assistants, labourers and similar posts.
    MN
    Management assistants, non-technical: clerical and office posts.
    MT
    Management assistants, technical: technical officers, nurses, laboratory staff.
    SL
    Senior level: doctors, engineers, lawyers and executive officers.
    RS
    Regulatory services such as Excise.
    U-
    University scales; the letters after it follow the same pattern.

    A scale written as "Rs. 41,800 to Rs. 65,560" is the basic monthly salary from the first year to the top of the grade. Allowances are paid on top.
