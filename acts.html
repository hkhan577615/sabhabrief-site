Check a document against an Act

For a notice, contract or letter that cites an Act: does the Act say what the document claims?

  1. Connect Sabha Brief to Claude. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and add a custom connector with this address: your Sabha Brief web address followed by /mcp
  2. Upload the document in a Claude chat and ask, for example: "Check this notice against the Acts it cites, using Sabha Brief."
  3. Claude looks up each Act and section in the official text through Sabha Brief and tells you what matches, what does not, and what to ask about.

Your document stays in your Claude chat. Sabha Brief only receives the names of Acts and sections to look up, never the document.

A sense check, not legal advice. It can spot a section that does not exist, a penalty above the Act's maximum, or an Act that has been amended or repealed. Ask a lawyer before acting on anything important.
